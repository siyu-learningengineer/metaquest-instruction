Step by Step

Step 1

Turn on the headset

Press and hold the power button to turn on your Meta Quest 3.

Step 2

Put on and adjust the headset

Adjust the straps until the headset feels secure and comfortable.

Step 3

Make the view clear

Turn the lens adjustment wheel until the image looks clear.

Step 4

Pick up the controllers

Put on the wrist straps. Point at an item and press the trigger to select it.

Step 5

Follow the setup instructions

Follow the instructions inside the headset to prepare your play area and connect to Wi-Fi.
