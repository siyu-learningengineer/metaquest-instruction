Three ways to view a digital world.

A headset, desktop VR, and 360 video can all show a virtual place. The way people view and use that place is different.

Simple comparison

Choose the format that fits the lesson.

Students do not always need a headset. The learning goal should guide the choice.

Format What students use What students can do Good classroom use
Head-mounted display A headset and controllers. Look around, move, select items, and take part in an activity. Practice, exploration, and lessons that need a strong sense of place.
Desktop VR A computer screen, mouse, and keyboard. Move through a digital place on the screen. Whole-class display, computer lab work, and students who cannot use a headset.
360 video A headset, computer, tablet, or phone. Look in every direction while the video plays. Field trips, observation, and short introductions to a place or event.
Key idea A headset is a device. Desktop VR is an experience on a computer. A 360 video is a recorded view in every direction.
1

Use a headset

Choose it when students need to act inside the experience.

2

Use desktop VR

Choose it when students need easy access on a school computer.

3

Use 360 video

Choose it when students need to observe a place from all sides.
