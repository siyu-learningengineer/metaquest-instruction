Introduction

Meta Quest 3 is an augmented reality device that combines the digital world with the physical environment around the user. It allows users to browse virtual worlds, manipulate objects in three dimensions, watch videos, play educational games, and work with other people in a more immersive way.

The difference between using a regular screen and Meta Quest 3 is that Meta Quest 3 helps users interact with the digital content as if it is a part of the classroom.

Classroom uses

Use the headset when it adds value.

  • Virtual field tripsStudents can visit places that are far away or difficult to enter.
  • Science observationStudents can study space, animals, weather, and the human body.
  • Practice and creationStudents can build, draw, speak, solve problems, and explain what they notice.
  • Shared reflectionStudents can describe the experience to classmates and connect it to the lesson.
Teacher reminder Check the app, space, time limit, and student needs before the lesson. Clean the headset between users.
