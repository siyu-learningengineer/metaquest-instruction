Meet Meta Quest 3.

Meta Quest 3 is a headset that places digital lessons and activities around you. Teachers can use it to support exploration, practice, and discussion.

Start with a clear learning goal.

The main parts

One headset. Two controllers. A safe space.

These parts work together. The headset shows the experience. The controllers help you select and move items.

1

Headset

The headset sits over your eyes. It tracks where you look and how you move your head.

2

Controllers

You hold one controller in each hand. They help you point, select, move, and hold digital items.

3

Play area

The headset asks you to set a safe area. Clear the floor before anyone starts.

Classroom uses

Use the headset when it adds value.

  • Virtual field tripsStudents can visit places that are far away or difficult to enter.
  • Science observationStudents can study space, animals, weather, and the human body.
  • Practice and creationStudents can build, draw, speak, solve problems, and explain what they notice.
  • Shared reflectionStudents can describe the experience to classmates and connect it to the lesson.
Teacher reminder Check the app, space, time limit, and student needs before the lesson. Clean the headset between users.
