Use the controllers with confidence.

Hold each controller without squeezing it. Point at an item and press the trigger to select it.

Controller functions

Learn the controls you will use most.

Some apps use the buttons differently. The app usually shows its controls when it starts.

Left controller

Use this hand to move and open app menus.

Thumbstick
Move or scroll. The exact action depends on the app.
X and Y buttons
Use extra actions chosen by the app.
Menu button
Open the menu inside many apps.
Trigger
Select an item when you point at it.
Side button
Hold or pick up a digital item.

Right controller

Use this hand for selection and the main Quest menu.

Thumbstick
Move or scroll. The exact action depends on the app.
A and B buttons
Use extra actions chosen by the app.
Meta button
Open the main Quest menu. Hold it to place the view in front of you again.
Trigger
Select an item when you point at it.
Side button
Hold or pick up a digital item.
Safe practice Keep the wrist straps on. Keep both feet still while you learn the buttons. Practice selecting one menu item before opening a lesson.

Quick practice

Try these three actions.

  • Point and selectPoint at a menu item. Press the trigger once.
  • ScrollMove the thumbstick slowly to move through a page or list.
  • Return to the main menuPress the Meta button on the right controller.
